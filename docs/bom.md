# Bill of Materials

| Reference | Quantity | Component | Function |
|---|---:|---|---|
| PSU1 | 1 | PLENTY COMPUTER ATX500WS | Fixed rails and standby supply |
| J2–J5 | 4 | Fixed binding posts | Red 12 V, yellow 5 V, green 3.3 V, black COM |
| J6–J7 | 2 | Adjustable binding posts | Module OUT+ and OUT− |
| U1 | 1 | ZK-4KX buck–boost module | Voltage adjustment and current limiting |
| F1–F3 | 3 | Fuses with panel holders | One per fixed positive output |
| F4 | 1 holder | Adjustable-section fuse holder | Shown in the positive output path |
| SW1 | 1 | Maintained switch | Connects PS_ON to COM |
| D1 | 1 | Green LED | Purple +5VSB indication |
| D2 | 1 | Red LED | Gray PWR_OK indication |
| R1–R2 | 2 | 1 kΩ resistors | Series LED resistors |
| J8 | 1 | 12 V panel-mount cigarette-lighter power socket with cap | Accessory output |
| J9 | 1 assembly | 5 V LED strip and connection | Enclosure lighting |
| M1 | Included in PSU | Original internal fan | Airflow |
| Enclosure and mounting parts | 1 set | Box, fasteners and supports | Mechanical assembly |
| Wiring and insulation | 1 set | Wires, terminals and insulation | Electrical connections |

J1 is the schematic's ATX signal reference. TP1 marks the PWR_OK measurement node; neither represents an additional purchased part.

Fuse selection and resistor power examples are in the [calculation section](calculations.md). The BOM lists the assembly components, while that section explains the design assumptions.
